# Shark ScrollWorld storyboard

The visitor stays inside the water throughout the journey. Scrolling downward moves forward; scrolling upward retraces the same visual path. These paintings are planning keyframes, not final frame-matched video.

1. **Put on the mask.** Above a sunlit painted sea, the mask reads “lets dive!”. It approaches the viewer; droplets slide on its lens. We cross the rippling waterline, enter the bright reef, and see the first [blacktip reef shark](../assets/shark-scrollworld-style-frame-v1.png).
2. **Whale sharks.** We follow the blacktip away from the coral and look up toward the surface. Several whale sharks pass overhead; the camera rises and arcs to see their [spotted backs from above](../assets/storyboard/shark-storyboard-whale-sharks-top-v3.png). The earlier [underneath view](../assets/storyboard/shark-storyboard-whale-sharks-v2.png) remains a reference for the approach.
3. **Hammerheads.** The view pans down into deep cobalt. One [hammerhead](../assets/storyboard/shark-storyboard-hammerhead-approach-v1.png) appears and approaches calmly. More may gather before it passes close and the camera follows its departure into darker water.
4. **Great white.** In colder, darker open water, the great white is revealed in stages: pale underside, eye, then [full body](../assets/storyboard/shark-storyboard-great-white-body-v3.png). We follow it until it disappears deeper below.
5. **Sixgill.** Near-black indigo water holds faint glow around, never on, the shark. A [sixgill](../assets/storyboard/shark-storyboard-sixgill-scary-close-v1.png) looms close in a scary but non-attacking encounter, then leaves.
6. **The tooth.** The camera descends to the seabed and discovers one enormous [fossil tooth](../assets/storyboard/shark-storyboard-megalodon-tooth-v1.png). The water stills.
7. **Ancient vision.** A [megalodon mouth](../assets/storyboard/shark-storyboard-megalodon-jumpscare-v1.png) suddenly rushes toward us. Its jaws close into watercolor ink.
8. **Suspense and return.** We hold in near-black water with [one bubble](../assets/storyboard/shark-storyboard-megalodon-suspense-hold-v1.png). The camera pulls back to the [calm present-day seabed](../assets/storyboard/shark-storyboard-megalodon-calm-return-v1.png), where the same fossil remains.

The first production test should connect the blacktip leaving the reef to the whale-shark reveal with matching source and destination frames. Test slow scroll, fast scroll, and reverse scroll before producing the complete sequence.
